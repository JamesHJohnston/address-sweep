# Address Sweep

Every place that needs your new address when you move house, found and ticked off.

- Pick where you're moving from and to. It covers the US, the UK, 16 European countries, Canada, Australia, and a general list for everywhere else.
- Sign in with Gmail or Outlook to scan your inbox for companies that have your address. Only the sender of each email is read, in your browser.
- Work through a checklist, with each item marked for before the move, moving week or after.
- Make a list of people to tell by post or in person, with ready-to-copy letters.

The site is one static `index.html`. It has no server, no database and no tracking, and nothing is saved.
