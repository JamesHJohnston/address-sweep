Find accounts in your email

Anyone who emails you a bill, a statement or an order confirmation probably has your address. Sign in and Address Sweep reads the sender of each email, right here in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or stored: there is no “us”, only this page.

You sign in with Google or Microsoft on their own page. Address Sweep asks only to see who sent each email (Google calls this “metadata”, Microsoft “basic mail”), never what the emails say. It looks at the last 3 years, up to 5,000 emails. The access is held in this tab only and ends when you close it.
